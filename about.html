About Rushikesh

Rushikesh Shirkar is a Senior Software Engineer and Product Engineer based in Pune, India. He builds software products end to end, with his deepest expertise in frontend architecture.

Background

He has more than six years of experience building and scaling SaaS, fintech, trade-tech and eCommerce products. His work spans the full product stack: interfaces in React, Next.js and TypeScript; services in Node.js and NestJS; data models in PostgreSQL; and infrastructure on AWS.

As the founding engineer at Dreamful Technologies, he took three multi-tenant SaaS products from zero to production: Eximfiles, the eBRC Platform and Scriphouse. He owned their architecture, backend services, database design and cloud infrastructure, and worked directly with the founders on product direction.

Career

  • Celoxis Technologies, Senior Software Engineer and frontend lead (December 2025 to present): owns the frontend of an enterprise project portfolio management platform used by global clients.
  • Dreamful Technologies, Founding Engineer (October 2022 to December 2025): built three multi-tenant trade-tech products from zero to production.
  • Spekond Technologies, Senior Software Engineer (October 2021 to October 2022): built high-traffic Next.js applications; Core Web Vitals, accessibility and SEO work contributed to a 25% increase in organic traffic.
  • Fashiostreet, Software Engineer (November 2019 to October 2021): built eCommerce and food delivery applications for web and mobile.

How he works

He treats architecture as a product decision, builds shared systems that make later features cheaper, and owns work through to production: deployed, monitored and holding up under real use. He uses AI-assisted development (Claude, Cursor and ChatGPT) to ship faster while keeping code standards and review in place.

Education

B.Tech in Information Technology, Rajarambapu Institute of Technology, Sangli (2015 to 2019).
