# Privacy notice

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and it does not ask you for any personal information.

## What this site stores

If you switch between the light and dark themes, your choice is saved in your own browser under the key "theme" using local storage. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

## Third-party services

- Hosting: the site is served by Vercel, which processes standard request data such as IP address, browser type and the page requested in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See the [Vercel privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- DNS: the domain is managed through Cloudflare, which resolves the domain name. See the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- Fonts: typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google servers, which receive your IP address. See the [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## If you get in touch

If you contact Rushikesh Shirkar by email or phone, your name, contact details and message are used only to reply to you. They are not sold, shared for marketing or added to any mailing list.
